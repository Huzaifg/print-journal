No. 06 / Original designs27 Sep 2026

Better seating. More to refine.

Controller fit frame · v0.4 · Gray PLA Basic

The revised frame is one fixed piece with mirrored grip cradles and a rear ramp that rises toward USB-C. The owner reports a much better fit at the handles, but only narrow line contact at the upper support.

CAD size
164 × 99 × 44 mm
Layer height
0.20 mm · 0.4 mm nozzle
Structure
4 walls · 15% gyroid
Supports
None · no brim
Material
61.22 g · slicer estimate
Print time
2 h 6 min · slicer estimate

The printer reported completion at all 220 layers without errors; supplied photos confirm the physical print. Time and mass are slicer estimates, not measured totals. Photographs show contact regions from oblique angles; exact gaps, controller tilt and contact pressure are not measured.

  • Quality

    The owner reports that the handles now sit pretty well. The upper support touches fixed shell but still makes narrow line contact, with an apparent gap in the photos; other reported problems await clarification.

  • Learned

    The mirrored grips seat better. Upper-post height sets the supported pose, while its face angle controls local contact; changing one does not settle the other. The photographed frame straddles a mat edge, so retest on one flat surface.

  • Next time

    Preserve the handles and one-piece design. The gap cannot be measured, so the next draft uses an explicitly assumed earlier profile and a modest added tilt. Test the contact and retention physically; the draft has not been printed.

Original model: designed in OpenSCAD by Codex with Huzaifa’s measurements and iterative feedback. Controller: Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller. · Printed on the Bambu Lab A1.

A fit test that changed the design.

Switch 2 Pro Controller fit frame · Gray PLA Basic

The first printed frame reached its real test: holding the controller. It did not retain it reliably. The owner reports backward slipping; the hand-held photos document the contact areas rather than a stable resting position.

CAD size
164 × 99 × 44 mm
Layer height
0.20 mm · 0.4 mm nozzle
Structure
4 walls · 15% gyroid
Supports
None · no brim
Material
59.09 g · slicer estimate
Print time
2 h 2 min · slicer estimate

Printed specimen confirmed by the owner and photos. Date records the fit review. Settings and estimates come from the saved print package; actual weight and duration were not measured. Startup purge can add material. Photo perspective does not establish exact gaps or tilt.

  • Quality

    The printed frame appears intact, but the functional fit failed: the controller slides backward. Photos show uneven seating; exact gaps and dimensional accuracy are unmeasured.

  • Learned

    Fitting two supports independently to a rough scan introduced a 2.63 mm difference at their centres. Agreement with that scan did not prove correct seating or retention.

  • Next time

    Test mirrored shallow grip cradles and a rear ramp that rises toward the USB-C end. A removable block and 2 mm spacers are planned for height adjustment; the revision has not been printed.

A little character. A lot of heart.

Mouse, bird & heart ornament · Gray PLA

Our first original ornament: two rounded characters, recessed eyes and a softly rounded heart on a shared base. The first physical prototype follows several rounds of design refinement.

Design size
110 × 46 × 56.5 mm
Layer height
0.16 mm · 0.4 mm nozzle
Structure
3 walls · 15% gyroid
Supports
Tree · 0.16 mm Z gap
Material
34.73 g · slicer estimate
Print time
2 h 1 min · slicer estimate

Completed, as reported by the owner. Settings are from the saved print package; size is from CAD, not a physical measurement. Material includes generated supports; extra startup purge may add to it. Actual weight, duration, base flatness and joint strength have not been measured or tested.

  • Quality

    Photos show clean rounded bodies and readable eyes and ears. Visible terraces on the feet, fine wisps and rough spots around the arm and heart tip still need attention.

  • Learned

    Even 0.16 mm layers leave steps on shallow curves. Raising the heart tip preserved its silhouette, but added a delicate area needing supports and careful cleanup.

  • Next time

    Remove remaining wisps and support nubs gently. Test finer or adaptive layers on a small sample, then review seam placement and support contacts before a refined reprint.

White-to-gray Benchy · White + gray PLA

The last of the white sample became the beginning of another boat. A filament-runout pause let us continue with the new gray PLA.

Layer height
0.25 mm
Material
10.99 g · estimate
Print time
22 min · estimate
Structure
2 walls · 10% infill

Slicer estimates exclude the extended changeover pause and extra loading/purge material. No photograph was recorded for this print.

  • Quality

    Completed successfully; the owner reports that it looks great. No photo inspection or strength test was performed.

  • Learned

    The runout sensor paused at layer 55. After manually loading gray PLA, the same job resumed; compatible PLA colors can bond as successive layers.

  • Next time

    Inspect any restart boundary on functional parts. Use an uninterrupted print when comparing surface finish, so the pause is not another variable.

Bed scraper · White PLA

The first useful print: a lightweight scraper, with a sloping blade that turned into a lesson about layers.

Layer height
0.20 mm
Material
2.95 g · estimate
Print time
14 min · estimate
Structure
2 walls · 15% infill

Material and time are from the saved print file, not measurements. Loading and startup purge use extra filament.

  • Quality

    Sits flat without rocking. The openings look clean, and the underside has a consistent texture from the build plate.

  • Learned

    A sloping surface becomes tiny terraces when printed in layers. At 0.20 mm, those steps are easy to see across the blade.

  • Next time

    Print the same design at 0.10 mm and compare the blade. Keep the material, orientation and physical shell thickness comparable.
