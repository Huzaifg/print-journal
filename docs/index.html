  • Quality

Photos are cropped to the fidget and metadata stripped, with no retouching. The desk photograph clips the spiral tip in the original.

  • Quality

    The owner says it is good and works, though the slide is not the smoothest and makes a slight sound. Photos show the assembled ivory ring and lilac spiral, with visible texture on the spiral. Sliding force and noise were not measured.

  • Learned

Stardock · Starship-inspired pen stand · White, green, lavender & pink PLA

From a cute rocket to a slender starship: a pointed nose and contrasting curved shield give the silhouette its character, while a rear opening keeps two pens accessible. Mirrored hull ledges rest on fixed lavender catch arms. Two white lattice panels print flat and assemble into a tower. Four separate color batches avoid repeated color changes within a print, at the cost of more assembly and glued decorations.

Date marks the assembled-photo report. Totals cover the four final plates, including generated supports and brims; they exclude the earlier 30.74 g joint test and extra startup purge. Actual mass and elapsed time are unmeasured. Four printed fins are not visible in these assembly views. Photos show two pens at rest, not a measured handling or stability test.

  • Quality

    The owner calls it “glorious.” Photos show the assembled tower, ship held above the base, two pens in the rear bay, and the pink nameplate and engine detail. Layer lines and a vertical line on the green shield remain visible. Exact joint gaps and glue placement are unmeasured.

  • Learned

    The small connector test supported keeping the joint geometry, but its supported hull-ledge underside needed cleanup. In slicing, “supports enabled” was insufficient: build-plate-only supports missed the shield opening. Allowing supports on the model produced the required scaffold. Review actual toolpaths and keep bearing faces flat during cleanup; appearance, contact fit and retention are separate checks.

  • Next time

    Keep the rear pen bay and mirrored catch contacts. Check repeated pen insertion/removal and gentle knocks on a flat surface. Trial support-interface changes on another small ledge sample before a full reprint; document the four fins once fitted. These are follow-up checks, not reported failures.

  • Quality

    The printed cap appears intact in the supplied photos, but it does not retain the controller: backward and downward slipping persists. Cap seating and exact contact area remain unmeasured.
